Membership

Terms & conditions

Chelsea Supporters Club UAE is a voluntary supporters club affiliated with Chelsea Football Club as an international supporters club.

1. Membership

  • Membership is open to all Chelsea supporters living in or visiting the UAE.
  • Members must be over 21. Under-21s may attend with a parent or guardian and must leave the venue by 20:00.
  • Membership fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Proof of membership, the physical card or the app QR code, is required to access benefits.

2. Conduct

  • Members and guests are expected to act respectfully towards other supporters, venue staff and the public.
  • Abusive, threatening or discriminatory behaviour may result in suspension or cancellation of membership.
  • Members are responsible for the conduct of any guests they bring.

3. Matchdays and events

  • The club organises official matchday gatherings, socials and special events.
  • Event tickets are non-refundable unless the club cancels the event.
  • Seating is first come, first served unless stated otherwise.
  • The club may refuse entry to anyone not complying with these terms.

4. Matchday benefits

  • Discounts and matchday deals are valid on official matchdays only.
  • Vouchers and drink tokens are valid for the stated period and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Outside matchdays, members receive 25% off subject to venue rules and exclusions.

5. Money

  • Membership fees and merchandise sales go towards running costs and event consumables.
  • The club is not a commercial business.
  • Membership and payment records are held by the club.

6. Media and filming

  • By attending matchdays and events, members agree to being photographed or filmed for promotional purposes unless they opt out in writing to the club.
  • The club's appointed media partner films matchdays and events and retains ownership of that footage.
  • The club may use that material on its social channels, in the app and in printed materials at no cost.
  • Any member may ask to be removed from a specific piece of content at any time.

7. Liability

  • Members and guests attend at their own risk.
  • The club accepts no responsibility for personal injury, loss or damage to personal property.

8. Changes

  • The club may update these terms at any time.
  • Members will be notified of significant changes by email, in the app, or on official channels.
